About

A fresh approach on trading through collaborative problem solving.

Since 2019

Humble since our beginnings.

ZeroPlus Derivatives was founded in 2019 after finding a unique opportunity in options and building a strategy around it. It has since blossomed into several strategies across a wide range of different products.

In the ever-changing world of financial markets, there's little room for hierarchy. Everyone has a voice. We employ new and experienced traders, software developers, and quantitative analysts to work alongside us, evolving our strategies and improving our processes.

Our Approach

Every great opportunity starts at zero and works its way up.

At ZeroPlus, we aim to remove emotion from the equation. Our data-driven approach eliminates risk as we add liquidity and provide transparency. We actively trade across all 16 US options exchanges using our own proprietary, in-house software to capture opportunities in US-listed derivatives both programmatically and manually.
